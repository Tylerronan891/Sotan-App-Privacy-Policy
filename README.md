# Sotan-App-Privacy-Policy
Privacy Policy for "Sotan Trap Simulator Demo" app

Sotan Trap Simulator Demo does not collect, store, or share any personal information. This policy explains what that means in practice. Effective November 18, 2026.
Information we collect
The app does not collect personal information. It has no accounts, does not ask for your name, email, or location, and does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.
Everything the app does happens on your device. Nothing you do in the app is sent to the developer or to any third party, and we do not sell or share data because we have none to share.
If you contact us by email, we will see your email address and message, and we use them only to reply.
Children's privacy
Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it also collects none from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.
Changes to this policy
If the app starts collecting any data, we will update this policy and the effective date above before the change ships. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
